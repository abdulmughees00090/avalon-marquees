PARKING & VALET LOGISTICS

300+ Car Parking & G.T. Road Access

Located right off main G.T. Road near Builders Mall, Avalon eliminates city traffic bottlenecks with an expansive paved private parking lot guarded 24/7 by trained security personnel and CCTV surveillance.
